# Confidential & NDA Case Studies

**Status:** Done · **Priority:** High  
**Sources:** Interaction Design Foundation (NDA case studies), Designcase / UX Playbook NDA guides, UX Research Blog NDA research cases.

---

## Principle

NDAs usually block **proprietary screens, exact metrics, customer data, unreleased strategy** — not **how you think**.  
Hiring teams care more about judgment than the client’s UI chrome.

Always respect legal/employer rules. When unsure: ask or omit.

---

## What to hide vs show

| Often hide | Usually still show |
|------------|-------------------|
| Exact UI screenshots | Role & ownership boundaries |
| Real metrics | Problem type (ops, onboarding, B2B catalog…) |
| Customer names, logos | Methods (interviews, instrumentation, tests) |
| Internal tool names (if sensitive) | Constraints & trade-offs |
| Roadmap / architecture secrets | Collaboration pattern |
| Raw PII | Anonymized outcomes & scale |

---

## Four practical tactics

1. **Generalize** — “Internal enterprise catalog / B2B workflow product” instead of full product dump  
2. **Sanitize** — % instead of absolute counts; redacted diagrams  
3. **Recreate** — Conceptual flows, funnel stages, event taxonomy, decision matrices (labeled as sanitized)  
4. **Defer depth** — Short public summary + “happy to go deeper in interview under confidentiality”

---

## Confidentiality note (template)

> [Product] is [internal / client] work. Proprietary interfaces and exact business metrics cannot be shown. This case focuses on role, decision-making, research, behavioral analysis, design approach, and outcomes at an anonymized level.

---

## Substitute artifacts (strong for Federico’s stack)

- Research plan / synthesis (anonymized)
- Journey or funnel stages (no brand chrome)
- Event taxonomy / instrumentation map
- Hypothesis matrix
- Decision / prioritization matrix
- Ownership split (owned / shared / not mine)
- Validation plan
- Measurement framework (without secret KPIs)

Animations/prototypes: OK as **interactive evidence** if they don’t leak proprietary UI; never as the only narrative.

---

## Anti-patterns

- Hiding the whole project because screenshots are forbidden
- Password walls that block recruiters entirely without a public skim layer
- Claiming metrics you can’t defend
- Over-fiction that breaks trust if the company is named on the CV
