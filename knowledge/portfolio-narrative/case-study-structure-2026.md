# Case Study Structure 2026 — Best Practice Synthesis

**Status:** Done · **Priority:** High  
**Sources synthesized:** UXfolio structure guides, Refonte 2026 portfolio guide, NN/IxDF-aligned NDA practice, senior PD interview patterns (STAR + ownership).

---

## What reviewers actually skim (≈30–60 seconds)

1. **Business/user problem** (not “I followed the design process”)
2. **What you owned** (vs team)
3. **One real decision + why**
4. **What changed** (outcome, even approximate)

If those four are missing above the fold, process depth never gets read.

---

## Recommended structure (flexible weights)

| # | Section | Must answer |
|---|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Context | Product, why it matters, constraints |
| 2 | Users | Who, job-to-be-done |
| 3 | Problem | What was broken / opportunity (behavior + consequence) |
| 4 | **Ownership** | Owned / shared / not mine — explicit |
| 5 | Evidence | Research + analytics signals (methods + what you learned) |
| 6 | Insight | The non-obvious finding that drove design |
| 7 | Options | 2–3 alternatives considered |
| 8 | Decision | What you recommended and criteria |
| 9 | Design | Flows / IA / UI as proof of decision — not a gallery |
| 10 | Validation | How tested |
| 11 | Outcome | Metrics, ranges, scale, or qualitative + honesty about limits |
| 12 | Reflection | What you’d change |
| 13 | Interactive | Prototype as **evidence**, not the story spine |

Senior signal = **ownership + trade-offs + influence + outcome**, not more wireframes.

---

## Role block (copy pattern)

```
Role: …
Team: PM, eng, architecture…
Duration: …
Owned: …
Shared: …
Not owned: …
```

Ambiguity about contribution = credibility hit (UXfolio / Refonte 2026).

---

## Two layers

| Layer | Job |
|-------|-----|
| **Skim** | Problem, role, one decision, result — under 1 minute |
| **Detail** | Evidence, options, collaboration, reflection — for deep readers |

---

## Outcomes without fake precision

Allowed patterns:
- % change when permitted
- Ranges (“double-digit”, “material drop in step X”)
- Scale (# workflows, teams, sessions, participants)
- Qualitative concrete impact
- “Exact metrics confidential; directionally …”

Forbidden: inventing numbers to fill a template.

---

## Count of cases

- Senior product designer: **3–4** strong cases beat 8 thin ones
- Lead with the case that best shows **ownership + outcome**
