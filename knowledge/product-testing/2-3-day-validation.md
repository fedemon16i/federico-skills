# 2-3 Day Validation Pack

**Status:** ADOPT  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-22

---

## Goal

Generate enough signal to de-risk a design direction or feature before committing significant build effort.

More depth than a 1-day check, still fast enough for real product timelines.

---

## When to use

- Choosing between serious design alternatives
- Validating a core flow before development invests heavily
- Understanding why a quantitative drop-off is happening
- Preparing evidence for a stakeholder decision

---

## Recommended playbook

### Day 1 — Setup & first signal
- Clarify the decision this research must influence
- Define 3–5 critical tasks or questions
- Set up unmoderated tests (Maze or similar) and/or schedule 4–6 short sessions
- Pull relevant existing analytics / session replays (Pendo, etc.)
- Launch unmoderated tests so they run overnight if possible

### Day 2 — Research execution
- Run moderated or follow-up sessions
- Review unmoderated results
- Do lightweight synthesis of emerging patterns
- Adjust remaining questions if needed

### Day 3 — Synthesis & recommendation
- Cluster findings by severity and frequency
- Separate must-fix vs nice-to-have
- Write a short decision-oriented summary
- Share recommendation + confidence level + what still needs validation

---

## Methods that fit well in 2–3 days

- Unmoderated usability tests (5–8 participants)
- 4–6 short user or stakeholder interviews
- First-click / preference tests
- Review of existing behavioral data
- Light tree test or card sort if IA is the question

---

## Output expectations

A good 2–3 day output includes:

- The decision that was being informed
- What was tested and with whom (n=)
- Top findings ranked by impact
- Clear recommendation
- Confidence level
- Open questions / next validation needs

---

## What Federico should own

- Framing the decision
- Choosing the lightest adequate method mix
- Interpreting severity of findings
- Final recommendation

Much of the execution (setup, recruiting support, first-pass analysis) can be delegated.
