# 1-Day Validation Pack

**Status:** ADOPT  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-22

---

## Goal

Get directional signal fast enough to influence a decision the same day or the next morning.

This is not about statistical confidence. It is about reducing the chance of building the wrong thing.

---

## When to use

- A design direction needs a quick reality check
- A stakeholder asks “can we test this quickly?”
- You need to decide between two rough alternatives
- Analytics show a problem but you need qualitative color today

---

## Recommended 1-day playbook

### Morning
1. Write the decision this research must influence (1–2 sentences)
2. Define 2–3 critical tasks or questions
3. Set up an unmoderated test (Maze or similar) **or** schedule 2–3 short calls
4. Prepare a very short discussion guide or task list

### Midday / Afternoon
5. Run the sessions (unmoderated can run in parallel)
6. Do a first-pass review of results as they come in

### End of day
7. Synthesize:
   - What worked
   - Main friction points
   - Recommendation + confidence level
8. Share a short update (even if incomplete)

---

## Tools that fit this pace

- Maze (unmoderated)
- Existing session replays (Pendo / Hotjar)
- 15–20 min user or stakeholder calls
- First-click or preference tests

---

## Output template (keep it short)

**Decision we needed to inform:**  
**What we did:**  
**Key findings:**  
**Recommendation:**  
**Confidence:** Low / Medium  
**What still needs validation:**

---

## What to avoid

- Trying to get statistical significance in one day
- Over-designing the study
- Not defining the decision first
- Waiting until “everything is perfect” to share signal
